## AL Tamil: language, literature and criticism: How should an A/L Tamil critical response begin?

Define the issue raised by the question and state a focused interpretation. Identify the relevant work and passage accurately. Develop an argument instead of beginning with a long memorised biography unrelated to the prompt.

## AL Tamil: language, literature and criticism: How can I analyse poetic voice?

Identify who appears to speak, to whom, in what situation and with what changes in tone or stance. Support the interpretation through the language of the poem. The poetic voice is not automatically a direct statement of the author's personal beliefs.

## AL Tamil: language, literature and criticism: How do theme and subject differ?

A subject is what a work is about at a broad level, such as conflict or family. A thematic interpretation explains what the work explores or suggests about that subject. Different readings must be tested against evidence.

## AL Tamil: language, literature and criticism: How can I compare two Tamil texts?

Use a common question or criterion, such as representation of a relationship or a narrative technique. Discuss evidence from both and explain the significance of similarities and differences. Do not compare on the basis of plot summaries alone.

## AL Tamil: language, literature and criticism: Why is the prescribed edition important?

Wording, selection, annotation and page numbers can vary across editions. Use the edition and syllabus required for the examination. Check quotations against the actual text rather than accepting a plausible-sounding generated line.

## AL Tamil: language, literature and criticism: How should grammar and style be revised together?

Correct sentence-level errors while checking whether word choice and structure serve the intended meaning and register. Read the revised paragraph as a whole. Automated corrections should be reviewed by a competent Tamil reader, especially in literary or formal writing.
