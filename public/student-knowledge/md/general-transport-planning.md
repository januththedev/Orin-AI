## GENERAL Student travel: rail services and practical planning: How should I check a train journey in Sri Lanka?

Use Sri Lanka Railways' official schedule service with the correct origin, destination and travel date where requested. Confirm the service and any operational notices before travelling. Saved study notes should not be treated as a live timetable.

## GENERAL Student travel: rail services and practical planning: How do a train schedule and a seat reservation differ?

A schedule describes services and times. A reservation concerns an available seat under the relevant booking rules. A service appearing in a timetable does not guarantee a reservable seat or current availability.

## GENERAL Student travel: rail services and practical planning: How can I plan a travel budget without inventing current fares?

List confirmed transport costs, meals and a reasonable contingency using prices checked for the actual journey. For arithmetic practice only: LKR 300 travel plus LKR 250 food plus LKR 150 contingency totals LKR 700; these are invented example amounts, not quoted fares.

## GENERAL Student travel: rail services and practical planning: What should a student check when travelling for an exam?

Check the venue, reporting instructions, route, likely travel time, an alternative plan and the required admission and identity documents. Follow official notices during disruption. Do not rely on a generated arrival time without checking actual transport conditions.
