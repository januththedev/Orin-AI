## AL Sinhala: language, literature and criticism: How does an A/L literary thesis differ from a topic?

A topic identifies an area such as imagery. A thesis makes a defensible claim about what the text does through that feature. Develop the claim with textual evidence, interpretation and attention to alternative readings.

## AL Sinhala: language, literature and criticism: How can narrative perspective affect interpretation?

A narrator's knowledge, reliability and relationship to events shape what a reader receives. Distinguish narrator, author and character. Use details from the prescribed work rather than assuming that every first-person narrator speaks for the author.

## AL Sinhala: language, literature and criticism: What should close reading of a Sinhala passage examine?

Examine diction, syntax, imagery, sound, rhythm, structure and shifts in tone as relevant to the passage. Explain effects in context. A list of technical terms without reference to the actual wording does not demonstrate close reading.

## AL Sinhala: language, literature and criticism: How can I improve a Sinhala critical essay draft?

Check that each paragraph advances the thesis, that examples are accurate and that analysis outweighs plot summary. Review coherence, formal register and language accuracy with teacher feedback. Do not present generated text as a verified model marking scheme.

## AL Sinhala: language, literature and criticism: Why does literary context matter?

Historical, social and literary contexts can illuminate a text's choices and reception. They should support an interpretation of the text rather than replace it. Avoid speculative claims about an author's private intentions without evidence.

## AL Sinhala: language, literature and criticism: How should I prepare for prescribed-text questions?

Keep an edition-specific reading log of key episodes, characters, techniques and short verified quotations. Organise themes across the whole work and practise responding to new prompts. This pack supplies analysis methods, not the complete prescribed reading list.
