## AL Home Economics and Communication & Media Studies: How can home economics connect resources and wellbeing?

Analyse how households allocate time, money, skills and materials to meet needs. Compare options using cost, suitability, maintenance and environmental impact. A justified decision explains its constraints rather than assuming that the most expensive option is best.

## AL Home Economics and Communication & Media Studies: How should I compare two fabrics for a particular use?

Define the use first, then compare properties such as absorbency, durability, comfort, care requirements and cost. Fibre type and fabric construction both matter. Follow the prescribed practical syllabus rather than inferring all properties from appearance.

Record: al-home-economics-media-002. Original foundation note; teacher review pending.

## AL Home Economics and Communication & Media Studies: Why is nutrition education different from a personal diet prescription?

Nutrition education explains nutrient functions, food variety and influences on food choice. A personal prescription must consider individual health and other factors. Use this pack for academic concepts, not to diagnose deficiencies or recommend medicines or supplements.

## AL Home Economics and Communication & Media Studies: What are the basic components of a communication process?

A useful introductory model identifies a sender, message, channel, receiver, feedback and context. Noise can affect interpretation. Different theoretical models emphasise different relationships, so identify the model used rather than treating one diagram as universal.

Record: al-home-economics-media-004. Original foundation note; teacher review pending.

## AL Home Economics and Communication & Media Studies: How do agenda-setting and framing differ?

Agenda-setting concerns which issues receive attention. Framing concerns how an issue is presented and interpreted. Analyse a specific example and support the distinction with evidence from the message, its selection and its context.

Record: al-home-economics-media-005. Original foundation note; teacher review pending.

## AL Home Economics and Communication & Media Studies: How can I evaluate a media message about students?

Examine creator, audience, purpose, representation, evidence, omissions and commercial or political incentives. Compare with independent sources. A high view count establishes reach, not truth or representativeness.
