## AL Religions and civilizations: separate contexts: How do religious doctrine and civilization studies differ?

A doctrinal question examines teachings within a specified tradition. Civilization studies may examine historical institutions, culture, texts, art and social change. Identify the actual subject and question; related names do not make these courses interchangeable.

## AL Religions and civilizations: separate contexts: How should Islamic civilization be studied historically?

Identify the period, region, institutions and evidence relevant to the prompt. Distinguish religious teaching, historical practice and later interpretation. Avoid treating events across many centuries and regions as if they formed a single unchanging society.

Use the prescribed historical setting, institutions, texts and cultural developments. Distinguish denominations and historical periods where relevant. Support comparisons and causal explanations with evidence rather than assuming all Christian communities developed identically.

## AL Religions and civilizations: separate contexts: How can Greek and Roman civilizations be compared?

Choose a defined topic and period, such as political institutions, literature or art. Use evidence for both and explain change and influence without treating all Greek city-states or the entire Roman period as one uniform case. Check the current prescribed scope.
