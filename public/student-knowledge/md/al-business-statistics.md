## AL Business Statistics: data and inference: What is the difference between descriptive and inferential statistics?

Descriptive statistics summarise observed data. Inferential statistics use a model and sample information to reason about a broader population or process. Inference depends on how the sample was obtained and on the model assumptions.

## AL Business Statistics: data and inference: Why might a median be preferable to a mean for incomes?

A median is less sensitive to a small number of extreme values. In a strongly skewed income distribution it can better describe a typical central position for some purposes. Report the measure and justify it rather than declaring one average always best.

## AL Business Statistics: data and inference: What does standard deviation measure?

It measures spread around the mean in the variable's units. Population and sample formulas differ in their denominators. A larger standard deviation indicates greater spread only when comparisons use compatible data and definitions.

## AL Business Statistics: data and inference: What is a sampling bias?

It occurs when the selection process systematically over- or under-represents relevant parts of the target population. Increasing sample size alone does not remove such bias. State the population, sampling frame and selection method.

## AL Business Statistics: data and inference: What does a confidence interval represent?

Under its procedure and assumptions, it gives a range produced by a method with a stated long-run coverage rate. A particular frequentist 95% interval does not generally mean the fixed parameter has a 95% probability of lying in that realised interval.

## AL Business Statistics: data and inference: Can a regression line be used safely far outside the observed range?

Extrapolation can be unreliable because the relationship may change beyond the data. Check the observed range, residual patterns, context and assumptions. A good fit within a sample is not proof of a causal relation or reliable distant predictions.
