## AL Mathematics: mechanics, vectors and probability: A particle starts from rest with constant acceleration 2 m s⁻² for 5 s. Find displacement.

Using s = ut + ½at² with u = 0 gives s = ½ × 2 × 25 = 25 m. These constant-acceleration equations require the stated acceleration to remain constant over the interval.

## AL Mathematics: mechanics, vectors and probability: How do I resolve a force F making angle θ to the horizontal?

Its horizontal and vertical components have magnitudes F cos θ and F sin θ when θ is measured from the horizontal. Assign signs using chosen axes. If the angle is measured from the vertical, the roles of sine and cosine change.

## AL Mathematics: mechanics, vectors and probability: What conditions describe static equilibrium of a rigid body in a plane?

The resultant force is zero and the resultant moment about any point is zero. Use independent force components and a suitable moment equation. Zero resultant force alone can still permit rotation if a couple acts.

## AL Mathematics: mechanics, vectors and probability: What is the scalar product of vectors (1,2) and (3,4)?

The scalar product is 1×3 + 2×4 = 11. It also equals |a||b| cos θ. For two non-zero vectors, a zero scalar product indicates perpendicular directions.

## AL Mathematics: mechanics, vectors and probability: What does conditional probability P(A|B) mean?

It is the probability of A given that B has occurred: P(A∩B)/P(B), provided P(B) > 0. Restrict the sample space to B. Independence would make P(A|B) = P(A), but it must not be assumed without justification.

## AL Mathematics: mechanics, vectors and probability: When is a binomial model appropriate?

Use it for a fixed number of independent trials with two classified outcomes and the same success probability on each trial. If X counts successes, P(X=k) = C(n,k)pᵏ(1−p)ⁿ⁻ᵏ. Check the assumptions before using the formula.
