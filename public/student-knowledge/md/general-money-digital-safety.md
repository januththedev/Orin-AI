## GENERAL Money skills and digital safety for students: How can I build a simple student budget?

Choose a period, list expected money available and all planned expenses, then compare the totals. If an invented weekly allowance is LKR 2,000 and planned expenses total LKR 1,650, the remaining amount is LKR 350. Revisit the plan when actual spending differs.

## GENERAL Money skills and digital safety for students: How do simple and compound interest differ?

Simple interest is calculated on the original principal under the stated arrangement. Compound interest adds interest to the relevant balance for later periods. For a hypothetical annual 10% rate, LKR 1,000 becomes LKR 1,100 after one year and LKR 1,210 after two annual compounding periods, before fees or tax.

## GENERAL Money skills and digital safety for students: How should I respond to a scholarship message asking for a password or OTP?

Do not share account passwords or one-time codes. Verify the scholarship independently through the institution's official channel, rather than through the supplied link. Keep evidence if needed and consult the appropriate official reporting guidance and a trusted adult.

## GENERAL Money skills and digital safety for students: Where should I check cyber-incident reporting instructions?

Use Sri Lanka CERT's current official reporting page or FAQ and select the channel for the incident type. Channels can differ and change, so this pack does not hardcode an email address or promise an outcome. Do not upload sensitive screenshots publicly.
