## GENERAL After A/L: university admissions and course research: How should I research a Sri Lankan state-university course?

Use the UGC admissions handbook for the applicable academic year. Check the exact course name, subject prerequisites, any O/L or other requirements, aptitude tests where applicable and application instructions. A friend's previous admission does not establish your eligibility.

## GENERAL After A/L: university admissions and course research: Does a past Z-score cutoff guarantee admission this year?

No. A published cutoff describes a particular selection exercise and category, and future selection depends on the applicable rules and applicant pool. Check the correct year, district and course. Never convert a previous cutoff into a guaranteed future offer.

## GENERAL After A/L: university admissions and course research: How can I compare degree options responsibly?

Compare recognition of the specific award, curriculum, entry requirements, cost, duration, location, support and graduate pathways. Ask the awarding or receiving institution about recognition where relevant. Do not assume that one recognition statement automatically covers every course offered by a provider.

## GENERAL After A/L: university admissions and course research: What alternatives can I explore after A/L?

Depending on eligibility and goals, investigate state-university courses, vocational qualifications, professional study, recognised higher-education programmes and employment with training. Check each provider's current requirements and total costs; this guide does not promise admission, equivalence or a job.
