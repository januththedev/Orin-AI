## AL Political Science: concepts and institutions: How are state, government and nation distinguished?

A state is an enduring political and legal organisation associated with population, territory and public authority. A government is the set of institutions or officeholders exercising governing power at a time. A nation concerns collective identity; the concepts need not coincide exactly.

## AL Political Science: concepts and institutions: What is separation of powers?

It concerns distributing governmental functions and authority among institutions to limit concentration of power. Actual constitutional systems combine separation with checks, balances and other relationships. Apply the current constitutional text rather than assuming every country has an identical arrangement.

## AL Political Science: concepts and institutions: How do legitimacy and coercion differ?

Legitimacy concerns a belief that authority is appropriate or justified; coercion concerns securing compliance through threatened or actual force or penalties. A political explanation can consider both without treating obedience as proof of legitimacy.

## AL Political Science: concepts and institutions: How should I compare electoral systems?

Define the systems, compare representation, accountability, voter choice and effects on party competition, and use appropriate evidence. Avoid claiming that a system guarantees one outcome in every society. Current Sri Lankan election rules require a current official source.

## AL Political Science: concepts and institutions: What is a political ideology?

It is an organised set of ideas about political life, values and desirable institutions or change. An ideology has internal variations and historical contexts. Explain a specified thinker's position rather than treating a label as a complete argument.

## AL Political Science: concepts and institutions: How do I write a balanced political-science answer?

Define the concept, explain its mechanisms, use relevant examples and evaluate competing arguments. Distinguish descriptive claims from normative judgements. This dataset is for academic analysis and does not tell a student which political group to support.
