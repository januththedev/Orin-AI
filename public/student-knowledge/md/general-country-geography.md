## GENERAL Sri Lanka: geography and finding reliable facts: How are provinces and districts organised in Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka has nine provinces and 25 administrative districts. The provinces are Western, Central, Southern, Northern, Eastern, North Western, North Central, Uva and Sabaragamuwa. For a dated statistical exercise, use the Department of Census and Statistics' stated administrative definitions and data year.

## GENERAL Sri Lanka: geography and finding reliable facts: Where should I find population figures for a school project?

Use Department of Census and Statistics census tables, record the reference date and distinguish final census results from preliminary reports and later population estimates. Do not combine numbers from different reference periods without explaining the difference.

## GENERAL Sri Lanka: geography and finding reliable facts: Why can two reliable sources show different population totals?

They may refer to different dates, geographic boundaries, definitions or methods. A census count and a later estimate are not automatically contradictory. Compare metadata before deciding that one number is wrong.

## GENERAL Sri Lanka: geography and finding reliable facts: How can I prepare a useful local-area profile?

Combine a map, clearly dated statistics, land use, transport, environmental features and observations from an appropriate field visit. Separate measured facts from opinions. Do not publish household-level personal information or identify classmates' homes.
