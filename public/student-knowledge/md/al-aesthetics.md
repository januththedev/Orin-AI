## AL Aesthetic subjects: art and performance analysis: How should an A/L aesthetic-subject practical be documented?

Record the task, research, original development, technical decisions, practice evidence and reflection against the prescribed criteria. Practical expectations vary by subject and year; this pack does not replace official assessment instructions.

## AL Aesthetic subjects: art and performance analysis: How can I analyse an artwork beyond identifying its subject?

Consider form, materials, technique, composition, context and interpretation. Describe specific visual evidence and explain how it contributes to meaning or effect. Attribution and dating require reliable sources, not visual resemblance alone.

## AL Aesthetic subjects: art and performance analysis: How should music from different traditions be compared?

Define the features being compared, such as pitch organisation, rhythm, instrumentation or performance context, using each tradition's terminology accurately. Do not reduce one system to an imperfect version of another.

## AL Aesthetic subjects: art and performance analysis: How can a performance evaluation be evidence-based?

Refer to observable choices in timing, movement, vocal delivery, interaction or design and explain their effect in the particular production. Distinguish personal preference from a supported judgement about the stated artistic intention.

## AL Aesthetic subjects: art and performance analysis: What is the relationship between theatre text and production?

A script offers material that a production interprets through acting, direction, space, sound and design. Analyse the specified text or performance rather than assuming that all productions realise the same choices.

## AL Aesthetic subjects: art and performance analysis: How should source material be credited in a creative project?

Document images, recordings, texts and other influences with creator and source information, and follow relevant permissions. Distinguish quotation, adaptation, study and original work. Access to a work online does not automatically grant reuse rights.
