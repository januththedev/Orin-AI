## AL Biology: inheritance, evolution and ecology: For a monohybrid cross Aa × Aa, what genotype ratio is expected?

Under a simple Mendelian model with equal segregation and random fertilisation, the genotype ratio is 1 AA : 2 Aa : 1 aa. A 3:1 phenotype ratio additionally requires complete dominance and the other model assumptions.

## AL Biology: inheritance, evolution and ecology: How are genotype and phenotype different?

Genotype concerns genetic constitution at the level being discussed. Phenotype concerns observable characteristics arising through genotype and environment. A dominant phenotype alone may not distinguish a homozygote from a heterozygote in a simple dominance model.

## AL Biology: inheritance, evolution and ecology: How do mitosis and meiosis differ?

Mitosis normally maintains chromosome number in the resulting nuclei. Meiosis involves two divisions after one round of DNA replication and reduces chromosome number, generating genetic variation through mechanisms including recombination and independent assortment.

## AL Biology: inheritance, evolution and ecology: What is natural selection?

Heritable variation can lead to differences in survival and reproductive success in a particular environment, changing population composition over generations. Individuals do not evolve a required trait simply because they need it. Distinguish individual acclimatisation from evolutionary change.

## AL Biology: inheritance, evolution and ecology: What is the difference between a habitat and a niche?

A habitat is the place or environment where an organism lives. A niche describes its ecological relationships and resource use within a system. Two species can share a habitat while differing in important aspects of their niches.

## AL Biology: inheritance, evolution and ecology: Why does energy usually decline between trophic levels?

Organisms use energy in metabolism, and some biomass is not consumed or assimilated by the next level. Transfer efficiencies vary. Do not apply a fixed 10% value unless the exercise explicitly specifies it or the data justify it.
