## AL Mathematics: algebra, functions and calculus: How do I differentiate y = x³ − 4x + 2?

Apply the power rule term by term: dy/dx = 3x² − 4. A stationary point requires dy/dx = 0, but classifying it needs further reasoning. State the variable with respect to which differentiation is being performed.

## AL Mathematics: algebra, functions and calculus: What is the integral of 2x + 3 with respect to x?

An antiderivative is x² + 3x + C, where C is an arbitrary constant. Differentiating the result recovers 2x + 3. A definite integral instead evaluates an accumulated signed quantity between specified limits.

## AL Mathematics: algebra, functions and calculus: What is the domain of f(x) = 1/(x − 2) over the real numbers?

The denominator must be non-zero, so the domain is all real x except 2. The range also excludes zero. Domain restrictions should be considered before algebraic cancellation, composition or solving an equation involving the function.

## AL Mathematics: algebra, functions and calculus: Solve x² − 5x + 6 = 0.

Factor as (x − 2)(x − 3) = 0. The zero-product property gives x = 2 or x = 3. This reasoning depends on a product equalling zero; a sum equalling zero cannot be treated in the same way.

## AL Mathematics: algebra, functions and calculus: How do I find the sum of the first 10 terms of 3, 5, 7, …?

This is an arithmetic progression with first term 3 and common difference 2. S₁₀ = 10[2(3) + 9(2)]/2 = 120. Identify whether a progression is arithmetic or geometric before choosing a formula.

## AL Mathematics: algebra, functions and calculus: Why are radians important in calculus?

Standard trigonometric derivative formulas, such as d(sin x)/dx = cos x, assume x is measured in radians. A degree-valued argument introduces a conversion factor. Check the convention in limits, derivatives and calculator work rather than silently switching units.
