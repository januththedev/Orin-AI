## AL Technology: engineering, biosystems and applied science: How should I distinguish ET, BST and SFT when finding notes?

Engineering Technology, Biosystems Technology and Science for Technology are distinct subjects. Some official archival directories also contain older technology titles. Confirm the current examination subject and syllabus rather than treating every technology name as the same course.

## AL Technology: engineering, biosystems and applied science: What is efficiency in an engineering calculation?

Efficiency = useful output energy or power divided by input energy or power, multiplied by 100% when expressed as a percentage. Use compatible units and the stated system boundary. Losses explain why useful output is smaller in ordinary real systems.

## AL Technology: engineering, biosystems and applied science: An ideal transformer has 1000 primary turns and 200 secondary turns. What is Vs/Vp?

For the ideal transformer model, Vs/Vp = Ns/Np = 200/1000 = 0.2. The voltage is stepped down. This theoretical calculation is not an instruction to build or handle a mains-powered circuit.

## AL Technology: engineering, biosystems and applied science: How does a biosystem mass balance work?

Account for inputs, outputs, accumulation and any relevant generation or consumption within a defined boundary. For a conserved total mass, input minus output equals accumulation. Specify the time interval and distinguish mass from concentration.

## AL Technology: engineering, biosystems and applied science: Why do food-processing calculations need both scientific and safety context?

Heat transfer, moisture and microbial behaviour affect product quality and safety. A classroom arithmetic result is not a validated processing schedule. Use prescribed supervised practicals and qualified standards for actual food production.

## AL Technology: engineering, biosystems and applied science: How should a technology prototype be tested?

Define measurable criteria, document constraints, assess hazards with supervision, measure repeatable performance and revise the design using evidence. Separate theoretical predictions from actual results and account for uncertainty and practical limitations.
