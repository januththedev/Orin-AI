## GENERAL Student public services: NIC, passports and documents: Where should I find first-time NIC application guidance?

Use the Department for Registration of Persons' official first-time application page and forms. Check current requirements, the appropriate submission channel and supporting documents. Avoid storing identity-card images or numbers in a public knowledge dataset.

## GENERAL Student public services: NIC, passports and documents: Where should I verify passport application instructions?

Use the Department of Immigration and Emigration's official website for current categories, documents, appointments, fees and processing arrangements. This dataset provides a route to the authority and intentionally does not promise a processing time or preserve a changing fee.

## GENERAL Student public services: NIC, passports and documents: How should I store school and identity documents digitally?

Keep protected copies in a trusted private location with backups and appropriate account security. Share only what the legitimate recipient needs. Redact personal identifiers in public examples, and never treat a public chat or shared study dataset as a document vault.

Record: general-public-services-003. Original foundation note; teacher review pending.

## GENERAL Student public services: NIC, passports and documents: How can I recognise an unofficial application helper?

Verify the official domain and the identity of anyone requesting documents or payment. Read the authority's own instructions before paying an intermediary. An official-looking logo or a social-media message alone does not establish authorisation.
