## GENERAL Student support: libraries, wellbeing and disruptions: How can I use library resources for an assignment?

Start with a school or public library and the National Library's official portal to identify relevant catalogues or services. Record author, title, edition and publication date. Finding a catalogue record does not necessarily mean the complete text is freely available online.

## GENERAL Student support: libraries, wellbeing and disruptions: What makes a manageable revision plan?

Define small topic goals, alternate recall practice with worked problems, revisit errors and leave time for rest and other responsibilities. Track what you can explain or solve independently, not just the hours spent looking at notes. Adapt the plan to your actual school commitments.

## GENERAL Student support: libraries, wellbeing and disruptions: How should a study app support wellbeing?

Encourage manageable goals, breaks, regular routines and seeking help from a trusted adult, teacher or qualified professional when needed. General healthy-food education can draw on reliable public-health sources, but a study dataset should not diagnose illness or prescribe medicines or supplements.

## GENERAL Student support: libraries, wellbeing and disruptions: How should I check exam travel during severe weather?

Check current Disaster Management Centre warnings, relevant transport notices and the examination authority's instructions. A weather or disruption warning must be checked live; a stored knowledge pack cannot determine whether a route or venue is safe today.
