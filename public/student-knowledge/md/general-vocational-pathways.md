## GENERAL After O/L and A/L: vocational and NVQ pathways: How is institute registration different from course accreditation?

Check both the training institution's registration and the exact course's accreditation or recognition status, including validity dates where provided. A registered institute does not by itself prove that every course it advertises has the specific accreditation you need.

## GENERAL After O/L and A/L: vocational and NVQ pathways: What pathways to NVQ does VTA describe?

VTA describes competency-based training and recognition of prior learning as routes, with requirements appropriate to the qualification. Use current TVEC or VTA guidance for the exact trade, level, assessment and application process. A short guide is not a certificate or an eligibility decision.

## GENERAL After O/L and A/L: vocational and NVQ pathways: How do I compare two training courses?

Compare the exact qualification, accreditation, practical hours, assessment, facilities, trainer support, fees, travel costs and progression options. Ask for written details. Avoid interpreting advertising promises about overseas work as a guaranteed visa or employment outcome.

## GENERAL After O/L and A/L: vocational and NVQ pathways: What should I ask before paying for a course?

Ask who awards the qualification, what evidence confirms current recognition, what the full fees cover, what equipment is needed, how assessment works and what the cancellation or refund terms are. Verify important claims independently through the relevant authority.
