Privacy Policy

Simplified View v4.9

01. Your Data Stays on Your Device

We built Orin Chat with a ‘Local-First’ approach. Your chat history lives in your browser, not on our servers. Unless you explicitly sync, you are the only one holding your data.

02. How We Use AI Models

We use Google’s Gemini to power the intelligence. Your prompts are sent securely for processing and then immediately forgotten. We do not use your data to train AI models.

03. Secure Connection

Everything sent between you and Orin is encrypted. No one can intercept your creative or professional work.

04. No Tracking Cookies

We don’t track you across the internet. We only use tiny storage bits to remember your dark mode setting and language preference.

05. Delete Your Data Anytime

Want a fresh start? Just log out or clear your browser cache. Since we don’t store your history, this permanently wipes everything instantly.

06. Voice Data Privacy

When you use Voice Mode, audio is converted to text in real-time. We never store or listen to your audio recordings.

07. Safety Standards

We follow global safety standards to ensure Orin is safe for daily professional and personal use.

08. Refund & Return Policy

Since Orin Chat provides instant access to digital neural services, payments are generally non-refundable once the service is utilized. However, if a technical failure prevents service delivery, please contact support within 7 days for a full refund resolution. You may cancel subscriptions at any time to prevent future billing charges.
